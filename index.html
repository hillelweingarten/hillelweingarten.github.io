Professional Background

Hillel Weingarten is an asset management professional specializing in macroeconomic analysis and real estate portfolio strategy. With a focus on acquiring stabilized, income-producing properties over high-risk ground-up development, Hillel applies rigorous macroeconomic principles to capture yield and protect capital across shifting rate cycles.

By focusing on existing cash-flowing real estate assets, Hillel's investment strategy prioritizes immediate debt service coverage, predictable cash flow, and long-term capital preservation against market volatility and construction inflation.
